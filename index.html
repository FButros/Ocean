Terms & Conditions

  • In line with Crown's Standard, Ocean 12 is dedicated in our pursuit of Responsible Service of Alcohol. Patrons arriving demonstrating signs of intoxication may be refused entry to our venue. In this circumstance, deposits will not be refunded.
  • Cocktail and Signature packages strictly begin at 8pm. Unless upgraded, both packages finish at 12am. Late arrivals will not have their booking extended.
  • Karaoke Weekend prices apply to Friday, Saturday and Sunday nights.
  • Karaoke Weekday prices apply to Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday nights.
  • Guests exhibiting signs of intoxication whilst in the venue, will not be served alcohol and may be asked to vacate the venue.
  • Ocean 12 does not support split bill in our venue; hence guests will not be permitted to pay individually.
  • Parties that exceed room size capacity may be automatically upgraded to larger room at the cost of guest.
  • Cancellations must be made 48 hours in advance to qualify for a full refund.
  • Guests will be held financially responsible for any damages they cause to the venue that require cleaning, repairs or replacement of equipment. This includes but is not exclusive to: broken or damaged Ocean 12 property, damages or disfiguration of the Ocean 12 venue, vandalism, the use of confetti/glitter, or theft of equipment.
  • All deposits are strictly non-refundable.
  • Increasing reservations is subject to availability.
  • Fees for credit card payments apply.
  • The valid period for all storage alcohol is 3 months, guests are required to show bottle card(s) for any redemption. In this regard, the right of final interpretation belongs to Ocean 12.
  • Patrons must be over 18 to enter our venue, please make sure everyone brings their ID.
  • Guests are not permitted to bring outside food, beverages or cigars to Ocean 12.
  • A 10% final bill VIP surcharge applies to all VIP rooms.
  • Prices may increase on public holidays and an additional 15% final bill surcharge applies on all public holidays.
  • Ocean 12 requests that guests notify service staff of any food allergies or intolerances prior to ordering, so that we can accommodate for those needs when preparing your meal. However, it should be noted that Ocean 12 cannot guarantee completely allergen-free meals due to potential trace allergens in the working environment and supplied ingredients.
